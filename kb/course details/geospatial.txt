# Geospatial Data Analytics (MDS581)
**Course Overview:**
This Trimester 5 Elective is built around transforming, visualizing, and predicting geographic and spatial datasets originating from hardware sensors, GPS, and IoT devices.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Introduction to Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
* Spatial data representations: Vector vs Raster data formats
* Coordinate Reference Systems (CRS) and Cartographic Projections
* Spatial queries, joins, and buffering using GeoPandas and PostGIS
* Satellite Imagery parsing and Remote Sensing computation
* Hotspot analysis and density mapping.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will engineer location-based AI services, enabling applications like dynamic fleet-routing optimization, environmental disaster prediction, and intelligent urban planning.
