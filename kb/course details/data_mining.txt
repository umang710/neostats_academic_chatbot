# Data Mining (MDS103)
**Course Overview:**
A foundational Trimester 1 core course focused on discovering patterns, anomalies, and correlations within large relational databases to predict outcomes.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD) process.
* Frequent Pattern Mining (Apriori and FP-Growth algorithms).
* Association Rule Learning.
* Clustering methodologies (K-Means, DBSCAN, Hierarchical).
* Classification techniques (Decision Trees, Naive Bayes, k-NN).
* Anomaly and Outlier detection mechanisms.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will be able to extract actionable business intelligence and hidden structural relationships from massive, unstructured data lakes.
