# Probability and Distribution Theory (MDS132)
**Course Overview:**
This Trimester 1 core course establishes the foundational understanding of random variables and statistical distributions required to build machine learning models.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Axiomatic approach to probability
* Conditional probability and Bayes' Theorem
* Discrete and Continuous Random Variables
* Common Probability Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential)
* Expected values, variance, and moments
* Joint and Marginal distributions
* Central Limit Theorem and Law of Large Numbers.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will confidently evaluate uncertainty and leverage probabilistic reasoning necessary for advanced statistical inference and Bayesian Machine Learning.
