# Image and Video Analytics (MDS583)
**Course Overview:**
A Trimester 5 Elective commonly known as Computer Vision (CV), dealing with how computers can gain high-level understanding from digital images or videos.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Image Filtering, Edge Detection (Canny, Sobel), and Transformations.
* Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) (ResNet, VGG, Inception).
* Object Detection architectures (YOLO, Faster R-CNN, SSD).
* Image Segmentation (U-Net, Mask R-CNN).
* Video tracking, Optical Flow, and Action Recognition.
* Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) for image synthesis.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Engineers will be capable of building visual intelligence systems for autonomous driving, medical image diagnosis (MRI/X-Ray parsing), and real-time surveillance processing.
