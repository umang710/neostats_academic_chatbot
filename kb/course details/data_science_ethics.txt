# Data Science Ethics (MDS105)
**Course Overview:**
A core Trimester 1 seminar dedicated to understanding the philosophical, societal, and legal implications of deploying artificial intelligence and automated decision-making software.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Algorithmic Bias and Fairness.
* Data Privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA).
* The Black Box problem and Explainable AI (XAI).
* Ethical implications of facial recognition and predictive policing.
* Intellectual Property in the era of Generative AI.
* Moral frameworks for self-driving vehicles and autonomous systems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will learn how to audit AI algorithms for racial or gender biases and ensure their data pipelines comply with rapidly evolving international privacy laws.
