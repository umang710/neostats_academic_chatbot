# Quantum Machine Learning (MDS572)
**Course Overview:**
A cutting-edge Trimester 5 elective introducing students to the next frontier of artificial intelligence: computing architectures that leverage quantum mechanics.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Introduction to Quantum Computing (Qubits, Superposition, Entanglement)
* Quantum Logic Gates and basic Quantum Circuits (Hadamard, CNOT)
* Translating classical data into quantum states (Quantum embeddings)
* Quantum Support Vector Machines (QSVM)
* Variational Quantum Eigensolvers (VQEs)
* Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) and hybrid classical-quantum models.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will master utilizing libraries like IBM's Qiskit or Google's Cirq to architect algorithms that simulate molecular chemistry or optimize logistics faster than classical Turing machines.
