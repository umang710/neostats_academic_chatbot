# Natural Language Processing (MDS463)
**Course Overview:**
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a specialized Trimester 4 elective designed to bridge the gap between human communication and machine understanding.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Text Preprocessing (Tokenization, Lemmatization, Stop-word removal)
* TF-IDF and Bag of Words models
* Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe, FastText)
* Sequence Models (RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs)
* Transformer Architectures (BERT, GPT, Attention Mechanisms)
* Applications: Sentiment Analysis, Named Entity Recognition (NER), Machine Translation.

**Learning Outcomes:**
By the end of the course, students will be able to construct production-ready language models capable of classifying text, translating languages, and building conversational agents.
