# Mathematical Foundations for Data Science (MDS133)
**Course Overview:**
This core Trimester 1 module provides the essential mathematical framework powering data science, focusing heavily on Linear Algebra and Calculus optimized for high-dimensional data analysis.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Vector spaces, subspaces, and linear independence
* Matrices, determinants, and systems of linear equations
* Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, and Spectral Decomposition
* Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
* Differential Calculus (Partial derivatives, Gradients, Hessians)
* Optimization: Gradient Descent and convex functions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will understand the underlying mathematical mechanics inside neural networks, optimization algorithms, and dimensionality reduction, ensuring they can read and implement state-of-the-art AI research papers.
