# Biostatistics (MDS563)
**Course Overview:**
An advanced Trimester 5 elective designed for students targeting the healthcare, pharmaceutical, and bioinformatics sectors. It applies mathematical statistics directly to biological and medical research.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Epidemiology Study Designs (Cohort, Case-control, Cross-sectional)
* Survival Analysis (Kaplan-Meier estimates, Cox Proportional Hazards)
* Clinical Trials methodology and Biostatistical Ethics
* Modeling health outcomes (Logistic regression for disease prediction)
* Analyzing longitudinal data and repeated measures
* Dealing with Right-Censored data in patient tracking software.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Graduates will be able to scientifically architect, analyze, and present medical studies with rigorous ethical boundaries, building AI systems capable of predicting patient deterioration.
