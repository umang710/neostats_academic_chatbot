# Evolutionary Algorithms (MDS571)
**Course Overview:**
A Trimester 5 Elective focused on biologically inspired metaheuristic optimization algorithms based on the principles of natural selection and genetics.

**Key Topics Covered:**
* Genetic Algorithms (Selection, Crossover, Mutation).
* Genetic Programming and building evolving code.
* Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO).
* Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) for routing problems.
* Multi-objective Evolutionary Optimization (NSGA-II).
* Applications in hyperparameter tuning and neural architecture search.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will be able to construct self-evolving optimization algorithms capable of finding global minimums in mathematically chaotic or non-differentiable environments.
