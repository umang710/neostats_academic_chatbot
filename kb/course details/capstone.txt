# Industry Capstone Project (MDS681) & Research Thesis (MDS682)
**Course Overview:**
Trimester 6 is entirely dedicated to synthesizing everything learned across the previous 5 trimesters into production-grade artifacts and peer-reviewed academic literature.

**MDS681: Industry Capstone Project (12 Credits)**
* Internships and massive-scale industry collaboration. 
* End-to-end Machine Learning lifecycle development (MLOps).
* Deploying a scalable Web API or Cloud solution featuring modern containerization (Docker/Kubernetes).
* Final deliverable involves a fully engineered application and a stakeholder presentation.

**MDS682: Research Publication and Thesis (8 Credits)**
* Constructing a novel algorithm, architecture, or data investigation methodology.
* Writing a mathematically rigorous, extensively cited paper formatted for IEEE or ACM journals.
* Defending the research methodologies to a panel of academic scientists.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Students will graduate with a massive portfolio piece proving their capability to orchestrate full-stack engineering and contribute original intelligence to the global algorithmic community.
